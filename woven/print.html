Why Woven by Toyota (ウーブン・バイ・トヨタ) (Nihonbashi, Chuo-ku, Tokyo (日本橋室町三井タワー))

Woven by Toyota is executing the most ambitious systems integration challenge in Japan: building Arene as a programmable operating system for millions of vehicles while turning Woven City into a living physical-digital testbed. That requires engineers who can cross cleanly between memory-safe Rust systems, deterministic 3D simulation twins, high-throughput cloud telemetry, and compressed edge ML — the exact intersection I have built across Osiris, pureQTern, MLX-QUANT, and 9+ years of production distributed engineering.
